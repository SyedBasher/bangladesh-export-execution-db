# Public versus commissioned data

BEOED is designed as a research infrastructure asset, not a one-off spreadsheet.

## Public research edition

The public edition should be useful enough to establish credibility. It can include source identifiers, market size, Bangladesh exports/share, market growth, supplier concentration, destination familiarity, product experience, transparent descriptive indices, documentation, and a reproducible sample.

## Commissioned / licensed layer

The following are candidates for commissioned or licensed access rather than automatic public release:

- model-estimated product-market entry probabilities;
- post-entry survival probabilities;
- execution probability and confidence grades;
- detailed competitor and tariff diagnostics;
- firm-capability matching;
- client-specific machinery/capacity/certification overlays;
- constraint-removal scenarios;
- domestic-value-capture and financing/exposure estimates;
- historical model vintages and forecast-performance records.

A consulting engagement can therefore combine the common BEOED infrastructure with a client's proprietary operating information without transferring the entire master asset.
