# Changelog

## v0.1.3
- Added five-year product-export persistence evidence.
- Added Bangladesh product imports, import origins, net exports, and log export/import diagnostic.
- Added persistent/recent capability-evidence classes.
- Added import-dominance diagnostic to reduce re-export/import-dependence misinterpretation.
- Added production feasibility archetypes separating downstream manufacturing, process industry, advanced technology, scrap/recycling, and endowment-linked products.
- Required persistent evidence for established/emerging market-gap screens.
- Split adjacent manufacturing into downstream, process-industry, and advanced-technology validation questions.
- Added recent-export-signal validation class.
- Excluded regulated categories from commercial opportunity-style screening.
- Extended QA semantics checks and public class-specific samples.

## v0.1.2
- Separated market conditions from capability and product-space adjacency.
- Renamed readiness index to trade familiarity.
- Added product-scale and destination-breadth information and more careful growth-base treatment.
- Renamed survival probability to persistence probability and execution probability to durable entry score.

## v0.1.1

- Made DuckDB portable and added formal QA/model-validation gates.
