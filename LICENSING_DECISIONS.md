# Licensing and redistribution decisions

## CEPII BACI
CEPII states that BACI is distributed under the **Etalab Open Licence 2.0** and that any use is authorized provided the source is mentioned. BEOED pins the source vintage and attributes CEPII/BACI in documentation and release metadata.

Source: https://www.cepii.fr/DATA_DOWNLOAD/baci/doc/baci_webpage.html

## Bangladesh Export Promotion Bureau exporter records
The v0.3 seed uses factual company/factory/product-registration information displayed in EPB's public Exporter Database. The public BEOED seed deliberately omits personal names, telephone numbers and personal e-mail addresses.

Before a large-scale commercial redistribution of an EPB-derived registry, Gold River Analytics should verify EPB site/database reuse terms or seek written permission if required. Until that check is complete, treat the seed as a research/provenance demonstration rather than an unrestricted bulk-resale licence.

Source: https://edb.epb.gov.bd/

## Tariffs, Gravity, TiVA and other enrichments
Do not assume one licence covers all v0.2/v0.4 sources. Each source adapter preserves source name/year and should have a recorded redistribution decision before its raw fields enter a public release. Derived research metrics may be distributed only where upstream terms permit.

## Commercial principle
The proprietary value of BEOED should come primarily from **manufactured diagnostics, model vintages, validation history, constraint analysis and client-specific execution layers**, not from reselling third-party raw data.
